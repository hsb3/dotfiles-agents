On the desk-platform data-model work (and IA generally), Henry requires **owner sign-off on any MAJOR information-architecture change before it is finalized or built** — new entity types, plane/structure reframes, relation-model changes, workflow decompositions.

**Why:** the IA is load-bearing and he owns it; these changes are expensive to unwind once built.

**How to apply:** present major IA changes as an explicit **approval gate** — a deck or owner-signoff, framed proposed-not-final — before folding them into the spec or building. Don't silently fold review findings or design decisions into the canonical model; surface them for approval first. Stated 2026-07-20. Relates to [[extender-estate-cohesion]].
