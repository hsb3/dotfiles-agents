Work cards the owner selected, scheduled, or prioritized; do not self-select from the pile.

Reproduce the current symptom before implementation and again before closure. If it does not
reproduce, record that result and identify the genuinely outstanding work before proceeding.

The kata claim, work-state, and closure mechanics are governed by [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).
