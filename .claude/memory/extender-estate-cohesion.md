The coding-agent extender estate is one system across three surfaces; only two still matter here.

- **dotfiles-agents** — Claude-Code-only marketplace today. opencode support is **deferred, not
  abandoned** (Henry, 2026-07-20): `gate:cross-tool` is a live promise, and the root `plugins/`
  layout will need revisiting when opencode lands.
- **desk-standard** (`~/Developer/desk-standard`) — the executive-desk governor, already
  multi-target (claude-plugin + opencode). **Deliberately not yet distributed from this
  marketplace** — it is under rapid development and Henry wants to avoid churn in both repos.
  Don't propose adding it to the catalog without asking.

The third surface, dev-tooling-desk, is archived at
`~/Documents/EXECUTIVE_DESK/Projects/ARCHIVE/dev-tooling-desk-old`; the desk estate now lives
under `~/Documents/EXECUTIVE_DESK/Projects/`. Its planning docs are historical — read them as
background, never as the current plan of record.
