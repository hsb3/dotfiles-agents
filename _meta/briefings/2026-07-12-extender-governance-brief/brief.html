Section 3

Where your two links fit

ECC is the closest peer at scale: like this repo, it renders one source into per-harness layouts and ships both a plugin-marketplace path and raw installs, hooks included. What it lacks is your curation layer — no provenance discipline, no promotion gate, no shelf model; it maximizes catalog size (278 skills) where you maximize proof. gsd-core is less a distributor than a methodology product (five-phase loops, fresh-context subagents, spec artifacts) with a smart runtime-detecting installer; its process layer competes with project-workflow's SOPs more than with the build step.

The strategic read across all 17: the moat is proven-and-provenanced, not the translator. As SKILL.md standardizes (~40 clients read it natively), translation value concentrates in what hasn't standardized — and D1/D2 are exactly the curation discipline none of these projects has.
