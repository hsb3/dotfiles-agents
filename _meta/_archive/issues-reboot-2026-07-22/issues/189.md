## Current state
- `.claude/agent-memory/` is **tracked** and hand-curated (MEMORY.md index + 4 topic files).
- There is **no `.claude/settings.json`** and **no `autoMemoryDirectory`** anywhere, so Claude Code's **auto-memory for this repo lands in the hidden machine-local default** (`~/.claude/projects/<slug>/memory/`) — NOT in `.claude/agent-memory/`. The tracked folder is a manual store; the auto store is invisible and non-transferable.
- The name `agent-memory` most likely arose from worker subagents dropping `.claude/agent-memory/` into their cwd (a known gotcha) rather than a deliberate choice.

## Options
- **A (recommended): adopt the documented standard.** Rename `.claude/agent-memory/` -> `.claude/memory/`, then `cc-project-memory init --portable` (writes a `~/`-relative `autoMemoryDirectory` to **tracked** `.claude/settings.json`, fixes `.gitignore`). Auto-memory then lands in-repo, travels via git, and the naming matches every other opted-in repo.
- **B: keep it manual + rename only.** Rename to `.claude/memory/` for consistency but leave auto-memory machine-local (no redirect) — if the intent is a curated-only store.
- **C: status quo** — close as intended.
