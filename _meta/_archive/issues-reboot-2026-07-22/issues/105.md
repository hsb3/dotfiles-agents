## What the audits found (65 items, re-verified 2026-07-14)

- **22 of 65 primitives are roster-labeled `origin: sourced`** — third-party content stored INSIDE
  `primitives-core/`: the 10 langchain-stack skills (langchain-ai), 4 openspec skills (Fission-AI),
  3 deep-agents skills (langchain-ai), framework-selection (langchain-ai), shadcn (shadcn-ui),
  skill-creator (anthropics), find-skills (vercel-labs), devcontainer-setup (trailofbits). All 22
  labels are truthful (real upstream + pinned ref).
- **Correction to the 2026-07-12 audit:** two vendored items DO hide in the `authored` set —
  **carbon-builder** (adapted from IBM Carbon Design System's skill; retains Apache-2.0, and
  `SKILL.md` says "adapted from an MCP-backed original") and **nanobanana** (vendored from
  `GargantuaX/openskills`; its README is the upstream's own, install command points there).
  **react-doctor** is unclear (21-line wrapper around the `react-doctor` npm tool; no upstream
  skill file located). Effective third-party scope: **24 confirmed + 1 unclear**.
- **Four plugins are 100% third-party content** (langchain-stack, openspec, deep-agents, meta);
  frontend-extras is half (shadcn).
- **The governing rule was undefined at charter level** — CLAUDE.md's "tracked, not vendored"
  language vs the sourced-in-core practice. Resolved by the owner ruling below.

## The ruled boundary (owner decision, 2026-07-14 — deliverable A codifies it)

1. **`origin: sourced` in `primitives-core/` is reserved for adopted forks we intentionally
   maintain and diverge**, each with a recorded divergence rationale. Everything tracked verbatim
   moves to `externals.yaml` by reference once #36 (clone-at-build) lands; until then items stay
   physically put and the ADR states the intent.
2. **Agent-generated content produced under the owner's direction is self-authored** for
   provenance purposes. Whether those items earn distribution is a usage question owned by the
   business-case retrofit / plugin-lineup ruling, not this issue.

## Deliverables

- **A — the boundary ADR** (`docs/decisions/`): codify the ruled boundary above. Charter gets a
  one-line pointer; CLAUDE.md/CHARTER language reconciled.
- **B — per-item disposition for all 24 confirmed third-party items (+ resolve react-doctor)**
  applying the ADR: adopt-as-fork (divergence rationale recorded) | move to externals (depends
  on #36) | retire to the salvage pile. Includes relabeling the two mislabels (carbon-builder →
  sourced/adapted with vendor + upstream; nanobanana → sourced with vendor + upstream) and a
  provenance verdict for react-doctor. Recorded on the roster (coordinate the mechanism with #39).
- **C — upstream-drift cadence:** pinned refs go stale silently; define the recurring upstream
  review step (the private-fork skill's upstream-review cycle is the SOP model) for whatever
  stays vendored.
