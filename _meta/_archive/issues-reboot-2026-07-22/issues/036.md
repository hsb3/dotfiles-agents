**Design deltas from the vault translation-service technical-design** (captured 2026-07-05 before that doc is archived — these have no other home):

1. **null-`upstream` semantics — a tension to resolve.** The TDD says a `sourced` entry with null upstream is "tracked, not buildable — the build SKIPS and RECORDS, does not fail." 2. **Hash mismatch at the same ref = LOUD failure** (upstream rewrote history) — distinct from a missing/absent pin.
3. **Separate network CI lane.** Clone-at-build needs network; keep the existing offline lanes network-free/required and isolate clone-at-build in its own lane so offline CI stays deterministic.
