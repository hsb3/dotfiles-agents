## Work order — desk-set restructure (desk decision 0020, accepted 2026-07-18)

Restructure the marketplace lineup around a binding naming principle, ruled by Henry:

1. **Desk bundles are named for the desk they serve:** `exec-desk`, `code-desk`.
2. **Anything that spans both desks ships STANDALONE** — its own plugin or catalog skill, never a member of a desk bundle.

The code desk is deliberately slim (next-release execution only); overhead moves to the exec-desk set. `hsb3/desk-standard` remains the executive desk's governor as a separate co-enabled product — not part of this repo.
