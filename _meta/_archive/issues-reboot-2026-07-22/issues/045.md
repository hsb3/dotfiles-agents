## Problem

The PLANS frontmatter audit and the staged-body sync tool contradict each other, so a staged `issue-body.md` cannot satisfy both:

- `repo-compliance-audit/scripts/audit.py:318-327` scopes PLANS-01..06 over every `*.md` under `_meta/plans/` (recursive), excluding only `README.md` and `_`-prefixed path components — so a staged `<slug>/issue-body.md` is REQUIRED to carry frontmatter.
- `_meta/plans/_utils/sync-bodies.py:67` diffs the staged file RAW against the live GitHub issue body (no frontmatter stripping) — so any frontmatter on a staged body reads as permanent phantom drift.

Live consequence: the (now archived) staged body for #41 carried no frontmatter and would have gapped on PLANS-01..06 at the next audit, despite being exactly the publishable body.

**Owner ruling (2026-07-02): issue bodies are exempt from the frontmatter requirements.** A staged `issue-body.md` carries the raw publishable body; the exemption is implemented on the audit side and `sync-bodies.py` stays raw.

## Deliverables

- A — `audit.py`: add `issue-body.md` to the PLANS scope exclusions (the filename filter at `repo-compliance-audit/scripts/audit.py:327`).
- B — Standard prose: state the exemption in the PLANS scope paragraph of `repo-meta-structure/references/checklist.md` and in `repo-meta-structure/references/planning-docs.md` (frontmatter-schema scope).
- C — Test: `tests/test_audit.py` fixture with a frontmatter-less `<slug>/issue-body.md` (no PLANS gap) alongside a frontmatter-less `<slug>/plan.md` (still gaps).

## Acceptance criteria

- Audit of a fixture repo containing `_meta/plans/x/issue-body.md` with NO frontmatter reports zero PLANS gaps for that file.
- The same fixture's `plan.md` without frontmatter still gaps on PLANS-01..06 (exemption is filename-scoped, not folder-wide).
- checklist.md's PLANS scope prose and planning-docs.md name the exemption identically (no divergent wording between the two).
- `make ci` green; `make build-check` clean after regeneration.
