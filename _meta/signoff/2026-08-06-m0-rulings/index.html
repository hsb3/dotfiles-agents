Design Gate 1

Bundle-composition principle (ADR extension): is it sound?

Task-9's design pass proposes an ADR extension defining when skills dual-home (appear in both a bundle + standalone) vs stay bundled vs go standalone. The principle is: a bundle coheres when every member needs another, OR when the bundle solves one end-user workflow atomically. See _meta/plans/task-9-lineup/design-pass.md § "ADR bundle-composition extension".

Recommendation: Approve the principle as stated — it's tight, composition-focused, and avoids conflating convenience with coupling.

Design Gate 3

Dual-home mechanism: accept shared symlinks as the implementation?

The design proposes implementing dual-homes as symlinks: the skill lives in primitives-core/ once, and two different plugin directories symlink to it. This is how ADR 0017 already works for standalones; dual-homing just adds a second symlink pointing to the same source. No copying, no duplication.

Recommendation: Approve the mechanism — it's the natural extension of ADR 0017, deterministic, and zero-cost at install.
